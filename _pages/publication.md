## Cartoon-to-real: An Approach to Translate Cartoon to Realistic Images using GAN

**Authors:** Sultan K. M. A., Rupty L. K., Pranto N. I., Shuvo S. K. & Jubair M. I.

**Venue:** International Conference on Innovation in Engineering and Technology (ICIET), Dhaka  
**Type:** Accepted Poster  
**Year:** 2018

The work explored transforming cartoon images into realistic images using **Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)**.

### Recognition

- Accepted as a poster at **ICIET 2018**
- Secured **1st runner-up** at **MindSparks 2019**
